What to make this page do

Five things on a menu, each with an Add button that currently does nothing. An empty order list. A total that says 0.00 and never changes. Your job is to wire it up.

The HTML and CSS are finished. script.js is already connected at the bottom of this page, and it is empty.

First, prove the connection. Put console.log('connected'); in script.js, reload this page, and open the console (right-click → Inspect → Console). If you don't see the word connected, stop and fix that before writing anything else.

Action 1 — Required

Clicking Add next to the latte makes the word Latte appear in the empty order box. One item, one button. Get that working before you touch the other four.

Action 2 — Required

Clicking it again adds a second line — the first one stays. Two lattes means two lines in the box. If your second click replaces the first line instead of joining it, that's the right bug to be looking at.

Action 3 — Required

The total keeps up. Every time something is added, the number next to Total goes up by the price of that item.

Read the price off the page — don't type the number into your JavaScript. Every price on this menu has its own id (price-latte, price-drip, and so on), and every total is sitting in total. Take what's there, add to it, put it back.

Action 4 — Optional

Wire up the other four buttons, then Start over — it empties the order box and puts the total back to 0.00.

Expect this, and don't fix it in a hurry. You add one latte and the total reads 0.003.00. It isn't a typo and your maths isn't wrong — JavaScript did exactly what you asked and the answer is useless. Write this one down carefully, all four columns. It is the single most useful thing you can bring back to class, and next week starts with it.
