Before you come in

Three things people ask at the desk every single day. We wrote them down so you don't have to stand in line to find out.

Catalog · Events · Ask a librarian

The short answers

  • Hours

    Open 9 to 7 Monday through Thursday, 9 to 5 on Friday and Saturday, closed Sunday.

    The book drop out front is unlocked all night.

  • Getting a card

    Bring something with your address on it. A piece of mail counts. It takes about four minutes and the card works the same day.

    Free for anyone who lives or works in the county.

  • The computer lab

    Fourteen machines in the back room. First come, first served, two hours a person when someone is waiting.

    Printing is ten cents a page, cash or card.
